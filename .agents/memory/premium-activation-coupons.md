---
name: Premium activation and coupons
description: Premium grant, VIP onboarding, and coupon redemption rules
---

Premium activation should go through one shared service so paid approvals, Stars, admin grants, and 100% coupons consistently update expiry, send onboarding, and issue the personal VIP invite. Coupon redemptions are audited per user and support configurable Premium duration and per-user limits.

**Why:** Multiple activation paths previously risked inconsistent VIP access and 100% coupons entering the manual payment queue instead of granting instantly.

**How to apply:** Keep Premium extension additive when an active expiry exists, make 100% percentage coupons bypass payment, and preserve redemption history rather than relying only on the aggregate used count.
